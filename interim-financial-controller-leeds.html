Why this brief

The brief is two jobs held together: own a divisional close that has to land accurately and on time into Group, and at the same time diagnose why the close is hard and fix the causes. I have done exactly that pairing twice. At Walgreens Boots Alliance I was Financial Controller and Head of Finance for the CDF International Division for five years — owning the divisional close, the trial balance review, the judgements, balance sheet sign-off and the reporting into Group, while running the simplification and cost-optimisation agenda across the same division. At Pepper Money, a PE-backed lender, I mapped the end-to-end transactional flows across the group, turned a GL and chart of accounts health-check into a prioritised roadmap, and delivered the fixes rather than handing over a diagnosis. Both are the shape of work this role describes.
