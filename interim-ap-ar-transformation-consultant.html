Why this brief

The brief is an AP and P2P transformation with a finance operations wrapper around it — supplier governance, payment controls, bank reconciliations, MI, and a team that needs its roles and accountabilities redrawn. Three engagements answer it directly. At Walgreens Boots Alliance I was brought in during 2017 to lead the SAP SRM implementation for the International Division, integrating it into SAP FICO and documenting the end-to-end P2P process; that work led to my appointment as Financial Controller of the division. At Pepper Money I stabilised and re-engineered P2P end to end and automated the AP mailboxes with Copilot Studio agents and Power Automate. At CMC and at WBA the O2C and credit control functions sat under me as Financial Controller, so both halves of this brief — the AP and P2P side and the AR and collections side — are ground I have owned rather than been adjacent to. Supplier statements sat inside the same P2P remit at both employers.
