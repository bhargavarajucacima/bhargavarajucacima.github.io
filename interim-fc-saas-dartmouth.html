Why this brief

The brief is a hands-on controllership seat: own the accounting function, own the close, produce reporting the business and its investors can rely on, and hold several priorities at once without dropping any of them. That is the shape of most of my last decade. At Walgreens Boots Alliance I was Financial Controller / Head of Finance for the CDF International Division, owning consolidation, management accounts, intercompany, capex, cash flow, treasury and statutory reporting under IFRS and US GAAP. At Pepper Money, a PE-backed lender, I re-engineered the month-end process and ran the balance sheet remediation programme that restored reporting integrity ahead of PE acquisition and carve-out. At Paratus AMC I ran controllership for the parent and 20 subsidiaries while supporting the CFO through the PE exit. Two things this brief asks for are not fully evidenced in my background — SaaS and covenant compliance reporting — and both are set out plainly below.
