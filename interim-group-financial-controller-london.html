Why this brief

The brief is a bridge role: take immediate ownership of group close, consolidation, balance sheet control, cash flow and the external audit, hold it steadily while a permanent Group FC is recruited, and tighten the reporting and control agenda on the way through. That is the shape of most of my last thirteen years. At Walgreens Boots Alliance I was Financial Controller and Head of Finance for the CDF International Division — group close, consolidated management accounts, intra-group profit elimination, capex, cash flow and treasury, with internal and external audit under IFRS and US GAAP and no missed deadlines. At Pepper Money, a PE-backed lender, I ran an enterprise-wide balance sheet due diligence and remediation programme that restored reporting integrity ahead of a PE acquisition and group carve-out. At Paratus AMC I was Interim Head of Financial Controlling for a parent and 20 subsidiaries, supporting the CFO through a PE exit.
