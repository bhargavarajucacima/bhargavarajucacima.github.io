Why this brief

The brief is senior financial leadership over a corporate and enabling services portfolio: the central budgets that fund everything else, partnered with Directors and budget holders, carrying revenue, capital, savings, business cases and major projects. That is the shape of the job I did for five years at Walgreens Boots Alliance, as Financial Controller and Head of Finance for the CDF International Division. My cost base there was central overhead, project cost centres and IT and shared services recharges rather than trading P&Ls, and the annual cycle was budget setting, cost challenge from the executive, savings delivery and investment appraisal. Before that, GSK and Commercial Metals Company gave me group reporting, statutory accounts and controls in equally complex multi-entity structures. My sector experience is corporate, listed and PE-backed, not local government — the note near the foot of this page says plainly what that does and does not cover.
