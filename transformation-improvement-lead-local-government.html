Why this brief

The brief asks for someone who can run a broad improvement agenda: redesign services and processes, drive organisational change, deliver programmes with proper governance and assurance, track benefits through to realisation, and hold senior stakeholders through it. That is the work I have done for the last decade, in large and complex organisations — Walgreens Boots Alliance through a $6bn divestment and a division-level transition, GSK through shared-service set-up and stabilisation, Commercial Metals Company through a European shared-service move and two ERP implementations, and Pepper Money through a full process re-engineering and automation programme. My transformation work is finance-anchored rather than whole-organisation service transformation, and I have not worked in local government. Both points are set out plainly below.
