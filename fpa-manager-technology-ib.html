Why this brief

The FP&A Manager, Technology brief is asking for a finance business partner who can sit inside a Digital & Data Office, own the forecasting and financial governance for a portfolio of large, multiyear technology investments, and give the CFO and Board a credible, current view of cost, risk and value realisation across that portfolio. That is close to the mandate I held at Walgreens Boots Alliance, where I was the finance partner for a £400 million multiyear IT cloud and data centre programme — including a $1.5 billion cloud deal with Microsoft — and, separately, a £300 million IT outsourcing project with TCS. I built the reporting frameworks, governance and controls those programmes ran on, owned a wider £300 million IT cost base across 90 cost centres, and led the recovery of a troubled £100 million project that needed a £60 million write-off. Most recently at Pepper Money I have been applying AI and automation to finance processes, which speaks directly to the exposure to AI in financial workflows the brief asks for.
