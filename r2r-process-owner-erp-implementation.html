Why this brief

The brief asks for a senior R2R subject matter expert who can sit on the client side of a greenfield Oracle or SAP implementation, own the process design, and hold the System Integrator to a solution that fits the organisation rather than the template. Three engagements answer that directly. At Walgreens Boots Alliance I was Process Owner for month-end reporting, fixed assets and intercompany, designed the solution for moving the international Controllership (R2R) function to an outsourced service provider, and was a key stakeholder in SAP ECC to S/4HANA preparation across all modules. At CMC I designed and led the implementation of Oracle Financials and then SAP across multiple European businesses, mapping processes and the chart of accounts from legacy into each target system. At GSK I set up and stabilised R2R inside Core Business Services, transitioning legacy processes onto the newly implemented SAP build. Most recently at Pepper Money I re-engineered R2R and P2P using Copilot Studio agents and Power Automate, and designed the Target Operating Model ahead of a new ERP.
