Why this brief

The brief is for a hands-on Financial Controller to hold controllership together while a c.£40m revenue division is separated from a £500m group, working alongside a project CFO and an existing carve-out team. Carve-out and separation work has run through three of my last four engagements. At Walgreens Boots Alliance I led the carve-out of the pharmaceutical wholesale business inside the CDF International Division as part of the $6bn Alliance Healthcare divestment, then ran the Transition Service Agreement with Cencora for 18 months afterwards. At GSK I worked on the Novartis asset swap and JV, and reconciled and closed three TSA projects covering divested products. Most recently at Pepper Money I ran the balance sheet due diligence and remediation that made the group ready for PE acquisition and carve-out. In each case the work was the unglamorous controllership that follows the deal — entity flows, intercompany, reconciliations, technical accounting and a reporting line that has to keep working while the perimeter moves.
