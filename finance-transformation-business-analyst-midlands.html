One thing worth saying plainly

I have not worked in the public sector or the not-for-profit sector. That requirement is on the brief and I am not going to write round it.

What I would put in its place is this. The regulated environments I have delivered ERP change in are FCA-regulated lenders, SOX-controlled corporate entities and NYSE and LSE listed groups — environments where the audit trail, the control framework and the evidence pack matter as much as the system. The analysis work itself does not change with the sector: mapping the As-Is, finding where the legacy process is holding the design hostage, agreeing a To-Be that finance will actually adopt, and getting a room of finance stakeholders to the same answer. I would rather have that conversation on a call than have it assumed either way.
