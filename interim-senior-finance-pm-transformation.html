Why this brief

The brief is for someone who can identify, prioritise and deliver finance transformation initiatives, automate the core cycles of month-end, forecasting and reporting, embed new planning and data tools, and stay close enough to the Finance Business Partnering team to be useful on day-to-day problems. Much of that work, in my case, has been done through shared service centres. I designed and executed the transition of CMC Europe's accounting and reporting into the Poland shared service centre; set up, transitioned and stabilised R2R into GSK's Core Business Services on a newly implemented SAP platform; designed and led the move of the Walgreens Boots Alliance international controllership into an outsourced service provider; and most recently at Pepper Money worked day to day with the group's service centre in Manila while re-engineering and automating the transactional cycles.
