Why this brief

This assignment has two halves: relocating a finance function from one country to another, and making it work as a UK function on the other side. I have delivered both, in different programmes. I designed and led three location moves — CMC Europe's accounting and reporting into the Poland shared service centre, GSK's R2R into Core Business Services on a newly implemented SAP platform, and Walgreens Boots Alliance's international controllership into an outsourced provider, where I led the recruitment of the receiving team at the partner location and onboarded and trained them. Separately at WBA I spearheaded the transition of the CDF Division into the Boots Division following the $6bn Alliance Healthcare divestment, which absorbed the CDF finance team and its processes into an established Boots finance function on both sides — the UK team into the Boots UK team, and the team at the outsourced partner into Boots' team at the same partner. What I have not done is run a relocation into the UK specifically. That is stated plainly below.
