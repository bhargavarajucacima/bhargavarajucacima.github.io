Why this brief

The brief is to take a new enterprise finance system from design through configuration, migration and cutover into a stable live environment, and to redesign the processes, hierarchies and Chart of Accounts around it. I have delivered that shape of work four times, and have been through system and vendor selection at WBA, Pepper Money and Paratus AMC: Oracle Financials and then SAP across CMC Europe, SAP into Core Business Services at GSK, Microsoft Dynamics GP Project Accounting at Inflamax Research, and TM1 / Planning Analytics end to end for the international division at Walgreens Boots Alliance. At WBA I also picked up an SAP implementation after go-live, took the open items to nil, wrote the process estate around it and later led the ECC to S/4HANA readiness work. Most recently at Pepper Money I ran a GL and Chart of Accounts health-check across 50+ entities and built the Target Operating Model ahead of a new ERP.

Two things worth saying plainly

The advert does not name the target platform. What I have implemented or led is SAP (ECC, and S/4HANA to the readiness stage), Oracle Financials, legacy Navision migration, Microsoft Dynamics GP, TM1 / Planning Analytics and Vena. NetSuite and Dynamics 365 Business Central are not among them, so if either is the chosen platform, that is worth knowing before we go further.

My systems work is finance-side. I have overseen configuration, testing and cutover and resolved what came out the other side, but I do not configure SAP FI/CO in SPRO and I hold no SAP functional certification. If the role needs hands-on configuration rather than programme and finance leadership, that is a gap.
