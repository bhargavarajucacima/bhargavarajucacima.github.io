Why this brief

A two-month interim Financial Controller assignment is about taking ownership of the close quickly, keeping reporting accurate while you learn the business, and leaving the function in better shape than you found it. That is the shape of most of my last thirteen years. I have run month-end, balance sheet control and statutory reporting at Walgreens Boots Alliance, GSK and CMC, and most recently spent a year at Pepper Money rebuilding the close, remediating the balance sheet and automating the manual work around both. Two short assignments are the closest comparators for the timescale here: Delphi Automotive, where a two-month backlog brief was cleared in 25 working days, and Paratus AMC, where I picked up controllership for a parent and 20 subsidiaries at short notice.
