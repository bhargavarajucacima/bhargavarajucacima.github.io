Why this brief

The brief is a safe pair of hands next to a newly appointed CFO: hold financial accounting and balance sheet control steady, tighten reconciliations and processes, carry a lean team through change, and do all of that while an SAP S/4HANA go-live lands in November. That is the shape of most of my last decade. At Walgreens Boots Alliance I was Financial Controller and Head of Finance for the CDF International Division and led preparation for the SAP ECC to S/4HANA transformation across all modules. At CMC I was Controller for a Fortune 500 steel processing and distribution business and led the implementation of Oracle Financials and then SAP across CMC Europe, including process and chart of accounts mapping. At GSK I set up SAP, transitioned R2R into Core Business Services and stabilised it afterwards. One thing worth saying plainly

The advert asks for hands-on S/4HANA. Mine is preparation-phase: I led the ECC to S/4HANA readiness work across all modules at WBA rather than taking an S/4HANA go-live over the line myself. What I have taken over the line is other ERP implementations — Oracle then SAP at CMC, SAP set-up and R2R stabilisation at GSK, TM1 / Planning Analytics at WBA, Dynamics GP at Inflamax — and the finance-side work a November cutover actually turns on is the same in each case: data and COA mapping, opening balance validation, reconciliations, cutover discipline, month-end stabilisation and controls afterwards. Worth ten minutes on a call so the CFO can judge whether that is close enough.
