Why this brief

The brief is a cover role with three defined deliverables: get the audit through, get the tax return done, and keep controllership running until the permanent Financial Controller starts. That is the shape of most of the thirteen years I have spent as an interim. At Paratus AMC I ran controllership for the parent and its 20 subsidiaries on an interim basis while a permanent appointment was made, covering BAU, statutory reporting and FCA filings at the same time. At Walgreens Boots Alliance I owned internal and external audit, statutory accounts, tax and VAT for the CDF International Division across five years and never missed a deadline. At CMC I owned statutory accounts under UK GAAP and US GAAP for the UK and Swiss entities.
