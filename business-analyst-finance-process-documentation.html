Why this brief

The brief is to document the finance process estate — P2P, O2C and the rest — so the business has a factual base for deciding what technology to put in next. That is almost exactly the work I finished at Pepper Money in June: I mapped and documented the end-to-end business and transactional flows for every group entity and stream, ran a GL and chart of accounts health-check across 50+ entities, and turned both into a prioritised improvement roadmap and a Target Operating Model built to prepare the business for a new ERP. At CMC and GSK I did the same As-Is to To-Be work with a platform change at the end of it — Navision to Oracle Financials to SAP at CMC, and R2R onto a newly implemented SAP platform at GSK.
