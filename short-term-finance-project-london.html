Why the reconciliations decide the audit

An audit query is rarely a disagreement about accounting. It is almost always a balance nobody can evidence — an aged item carried forward because it never got investigated, an intercompany position two entities book differently, a suspense account that quietly absorbed the difference. Reconcile it and the query closes itself. Leave it and it becomes an adjustment, a management letter point, or a caveat in a due diligence report at exactly the wrong moment. That is why I work the reconciliation first and the query second, and why I ask for the ageing before I ask for anything else.
